About

I chose fashion tech for my thesis because it’s the industry I want to break into.

When I started researching it, I went looking for a single resource that could tell me everything I needed to know: who the companies are, what they build, how they raise money, and what gets in their way. I couldn’t find one.

The information existed, but it was scattered. All Things Fashion Tech covers the sector in its newsletter. Fashion & Founders The Podcast interviews the people building companies in the space. Each was useful on its own, but there was no place that brought all of it together. To understand the industry, I had to piece it together myself.

Fashion Tech Index is my attempt to build that place: one platform that compiles what anyone curious about fashion tech, or hoping to work in it, would want to know.

Resources that helped me start

  • All Things Fashion TechA newsletter and community covering fashion tech companies, news, and jobs.
  • Fashion & Founders The PodcastConversations with founders and operators across fashion, tech, and circularity.

About me

I’m Sana Tahir, a master’s candidate in Media Innovation and Data Communication at Northeastern University, graduating in December 2026. Before that, I was a business and finance reporter at Forbes Middle East, wrote features for The Express Tribune, and freelanced for Dawn.

I’ve also worked on the other side of fashion: I ran a small styling consultancy, worked as a bridal stylist for South Asian women around the world, and write about fashion on Instagram. This project is where those two halves of my work meet.
