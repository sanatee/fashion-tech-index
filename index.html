Definition

fashion tech

noun fash·ion tech \ ˈfa-shən ˈtek \

also fashion technology or fashtech

  1. 1 : the intersection between fashion and technology
  2. 2 : an interdisciplinary field that brings fashion and textiles together with electronics, software, and other modern technologies Fashion tech companies are changing how people think about, view, and consume fashion.

Sources

Sense 1: “What Is Fashion Tech?”, All Things Fashion Tech.

Sense 2: adapted from Joan Horvath, Lyn Hoge, and Rich Cameron, Practical Fashion Tech (Apress, 2016).

Variant spelling “fashtech”: WTVOX. Example sentence from this site’s blog.
